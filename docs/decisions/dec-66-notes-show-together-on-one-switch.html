DEC-66 — notes show together, on one switch

What explains a line on the setup screen is a note, drawn under that line while the screen's one switch is on, and absent while it is off. No line carries a control of its own for it. The switch starts off, and the shell remembers the choice.

Decision. The setup screen has one notes switch, the help glyph (a ? in a circle) at the right end of the device row. It reads show notes, then hide notes, and announces whether the notes are shown. While it is on, every note on the screen is drawn under the line it explains. While it is off, none is on the page. The choice starts off, holds across platforms, screens and launches, and the quit dialog's notes follow it.
