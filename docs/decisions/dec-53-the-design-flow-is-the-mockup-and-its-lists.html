DEC-53 — the design flow is the mockup and its lists

Six staged artifacts, two loop-backs, a five-step close, a 17-row receipt and an adversarial finder were built so an agent would think before it drew. Fifteen closed runs say the human reviewing the mockup was the gate every time, and every artifact upstream of the mockup was re-edited after each round to stay consistent with it. What survives is the mockup and the lists that record what the screen was handed, what keys drive it, what it proposes, what blocked it and what the human said.

Decided 2026-09-17 · E-SIMPDF.W1 · active · amends DEC-14, DEC-15, DEC-24, DEC-34, DEC-46 · supersedes DEC-40 · the corpus scan it rests on is the E-SIMPDF plan v2.2 (T3, archived at the epic's close)

The decision

A design run is one mockup doc: the Given list, the mounted stateful mockup, a Keyboard list, proposals, blocked-on and review — mounted from the registry, judged by Tier-1 on every write and by the human at the mockup, closed in three steps, and handed to the build by the accounting alone. The Frame, Layout, Elements and Interaction stages, the Tune table, both loop-backs, the receipt, the soft-rules table, the rulebook pin, the predecessor walk and the Tier-3 finder are cut. No rule is added, no slug renamed, no mockup-bound predicate relaxed. This is a scope call, not a cost optimisation: the numbers below are the record that the cut stations never carried the quality — the review rows did — kept beside each cut so it cannot be re-bought without new evidence (DEC-50's habit).

The retired row leaves the table with its predicate, not before. The checker's drift alarm (xtask/src/doc.rs, every hard rule in the live rulebook carries a predicate) reads the live rulebook and refuses a predicate whose rule the book no longer states. So this record's WU marks the row retired — DEC-53 — the rulebook's deprecate-with-a-pointer rule for a slug past runs cited — and E-SIMPDF.W2 deletes row and predicate in one commit. Until then the checker still reads the cut stages; the record leads the code by one Working Unit, by design.
