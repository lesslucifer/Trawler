DEC-48 — a round is bought on the clock and the bill

E-SIMPWF.W8 gives a review round an instrument and takes the cuts that cost nothing to take: Gate 0 leaves the critical path, and the fleet's shape is fixed by rule so a later reading of "fewer agents" cannot re-buy a shape W6 already measured and refused.

Decided 2026-09-14 · E-SIMPWF.W8 · active

The decision

A round records what it cost. The cr/v5 note's fleet: block carries stage_seconds (gate 0, finders, verifiers, total), candidates by severity, verifier_model and shards. An absent row is a reading nobody took, never a measured zero — the same discipline W6 landed for the block as a whole. No gate check reads any of it: the tally is local measurement, never evidence (W1's ruling, unchanged).

Gate 0 runs beside the finder stage, not in front of it. cargo xtask preflight … --defer-gate0 settles the cheap half — the checkout is the reviewed tip, nothing is uncommitted, the notes the finders read are on it — and returns without measuring the tree; cargo xtask gate0 is spawned in the same message as the five finders. A red Gate 0 voids the round outright: no verdict, no note, nothing the fleet returned is written down.

Finders are never merged and verifiers are never batched. Five dimensions, five agents; one candidate, one fresh agent. This is a rule rather than a default, because both folds look like savings and neither is one.

Why it needed deciding

W6 measured two ways to shrink the fleet and its own measurement refused both. The reading that followed — that the round trades clock against tokens — was an artifact of which cuts had been measured: both folds moved work between stages rather than removing it. The rule below is what separates the two kinds of cut.

A cut that moves work between stages buys one axis with the other. Only removing work takes both. The work does not vanish with the agent: it moves into the agents that remain, which then read more and think longer.

Taking it now would be W6's mistake with the sign flipped
