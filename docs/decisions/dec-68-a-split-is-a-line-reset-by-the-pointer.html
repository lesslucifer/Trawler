DEC-68 — a split is a line, reset by the pointer

What a user-sized boundary looks like, how it is reset, and what unit it speaks: the three forks of the Splitter design, kept here when the design file retired into the component's TSDoc (DEC-16).

v1 · 2026-11-03 · Trawler · T2 decision · minted by E-PANELS.W3 from the splitters' design run (owner's round 1, C4 and C5) · within DEC-67 · UC-4 · UC-5 · active

Decision. A user-sized boundary is the 1 px line the two zones already shared, with a transparent hit band reaching grip-reach past it on each side. A double-click resets that one boundary; no key does. The value is the caller's unit, with pixels converted by the caller's ratio.

The forks

ForkSettledRejected, and why
affordancea 9 px transparent band over the 1 px line; the line lifts from hairline to ink-mute on hover and drag; the resize cursor says the resta visible 8 px gutter with a grip glyph: chrome on every split for what the cursor already says, and a screen that no longer looks the same at rest
reset keynone (owner, C5: "no need keyboard involved"). The arrows and Home / End reach every value, the default included; View ▸ Reset Layout resets allEnter to reset: a second meaning for a key the rest of the app uses to act · Enter to collapse, as ARIA's window-splitter example does: nothing collapses here (DEC-67)
unitthe caller's: pixels for a width, a share for the headers, with perPx converting a dragged or stepped pixelalways pixels: the headers split is a share of the pane, so it would convert twice and drift as the pane's height changes

Consequences
