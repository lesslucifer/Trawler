DEC-33 — this machine and the wire are two layers; a verdict never borrows across them, and traffic is judged against a clock

A probe knows the machine at the moment it ran. A captured flow knows the wire at the moment it was captured. Neither knows the other, and neither knows now unless it carries a time.

DEC-33 · 2026-09-06 · Trawler · E-REDESIGN.W10 · from the owner's review of the running setup screen, distilled with design run #9's amendment · status: active · amends DEC-13's connection-signal clause · amended by DEC-62 · DEC-63

Context

E-UISETUP.W6 gave each setup step "two truths": a local probe or observed traffic, either one turning the step green. The observation was a pair of booleans — decrypted, trafficSeen — with no time attached. On the running build the owner turned the proxy off, came back to the setup screen, and found every step still green: a flow captured an hour earlier was vouching for the proxy, the certificate and the capture, and nothing on screen could say otherwise until something asked the machine again.

The defect was not a stale value. It was a verdict built from evidence that could not support it. A decrypted flow proves that some client trusted the certificate and routed here at that moment. It proves nothing about this machine's trust store, nothing about this machine's proxy setting, and nothing about now.

Options rejected

OptionWhy not
Keep "two truths" and put a recency window on the borrowed positivea recent decrypted flow still says nothing about this machine — a phone that trusts the certificate produces it with the host untouched. The borrowing was the defect, not its age
A per-step "last confirmed at" stamp, one verdict per stepkeeps three verdicts arguing over one fact; the screen still cannot show "not on this machine" beside "capturing", which is the true state of the remote case
Relative time on the verdict — "capturing · 4 min ago"DEC-22 makes absolute the default for a reason a debugging tool shares: the user compares the stamp with their own clock and logs. Recency is the verdict's tone; the time is a fact
No window — "capturing" whenever any flow exists, with the time beside itthis is what shipped, and it is what the owner reported: a green that outlives its evidence is the false green Frame A names as costing an hour
Re-probe on an intervaltwo shell commands per tick, forever, to answer a question that changes when the user acts. Re-reading on the action, on demand, and on window focus covers every moment the answer can move
Timestamp the tunnel tally in the corea core change for a UI verdict; the live event already carries its arrival time, and the seed's only remaining job — idle versus waiting — needs no clock

Consequences

  • DEC-13's connection-signal clause — "so the proxy step can go green on any tunnel" — is superseded; the record carries a dated note. The signal, the endpoint and the event are unchanged.
  • Amended 2026-09-30 by DEC-62: where no probe answers, This machine's two tasks read the one request the app made of its own, marked and captured decrypted this session — never any other traffic. The probes are re-read on a 3 s watch while setup shows, the interval this record rejected at 270 ms a read; the readers now take about 20 ms.
  • Amended 2026-10-23 by DEC-63: another device's tasks read only traffic that started after its address was allowed, and only its own marked request wherever this machine's proxy points here or cannot be read. Each proof carries its time and lasts the session.
  • trustStatus and proxyStatus take no traffic input; there is no argument through which old evidence could reach them. trafficStatus replaces verifyStatus and takes the timestamps and now. A future surface that wants a step to go green on traffic argues with this record first.
  • UC-ONBOARD's contract reads "two layers of status, never blended" in place of "two-truths status per step".
  • Design run #9 was amended inside the build WU under DEC-24/25: its Frame gained three constraints, its Layout a zone, and its short table went from sixteen to nineteen. The amended blob is what the build cites.
