DEC-49 — a parallel stage costs its slowest member

E-SIMPWF.W7 proposed re-running only the dimensions a corrective fix can reach. It is rejected, and the reason generalises: removing members from a parallel stage buys tokens and leaves the clock alone. Every later "fewer agents" proposal owes this test before it owes an A/B.

Decided 2026-09-15 · E-SIMPWF.W7 · active

The decision

The rule. A cut that removes members from a stage whose agents run in parallel buys tokens, never wall-clock — the stage costs its slowest member, so the clock moves only if the member removed is the slowest one. A proposal to shrink the fleet must say which member it removes and whether that member is the slowest, before it is worth measuring at all.

This is the companion to DEC-48's rule that a cut which moves work between stages buys one axis with the other. Together they cover both ways a round can be made smaller: moving work, and removing agents.

Why it needed deciding

"Don't re-run what the fix cannot have broken" is an obvious-sounding economy, and it will be proposed again. It was registered in the E-SIMPWF plan as a droppable experiment, and the four readings that decided it are not reachable by intuition.

Rejected, with the reason that rejected it

AlternativeWhy not
Re-run the dimensions whose prior findings touched the delta's files A dimension that found nothing in round 1 has no files to compare against, and the new hunk is exactly where it might now find something
Reuse a dimension's verdict when the delta is small Size is not safety. A one-line corrective fix is where a reuse or altitude defect appears, because it was written under pressure to satisfy one blocker
Reuse a verifier's verdict for a byte-identical claim The soundest variant and still unsafe — a refutation rests on callers the delta may have moved. It also buys no clock: the verifier stage is parallel too, so this rule applies to it unchanged
Adopt it for tokens alone, accepting no clock saving It would fire on ~11% of rounds and would make a note claim more than its round judged, against DEC-45. The bill is not worth the binding
Build it first and measure after Three of the four readings come from the notes and git history at no agent cost. Building would have spent the fleet to reach the same verdict
