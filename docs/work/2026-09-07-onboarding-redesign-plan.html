E-ONBOARD — epic plan skeleton

Re-design and rebuild the setup screen and the quit dialog — the pair E-REDESIGN shipped gated, not endorsed, and the one piece of work E-RFINISH handed out rather than absorbed.

draft v0 · 2026-09-07 · Trawler · T3 (docs/work/) · branch epic/onboarding-redesign
This is a skeleton, deliberately. It exists so the deferral has a home in the registry — the epic is opened, not started. It carries the intent, what it inherits and what is still undecided; the checklist items a started epic owes (use-cases · expected · WU summaries · e2e plan · distillation plan) are listed at the bottom and written when the epic actually begins. It stays data-status="draft" until then, which is what keeps verify-doc silent about the gaps rather than lying about them.

Intent

E-REDESIGN.W10 shipped Onboarding.tsx (563 lines) and ProxyCloseDialog.tsx (288) after eleven review rounds and three owner rounds, and its own result declined to endorse the pair as final: the last owner round found the status model wrong at the root and produced DEC-33, which fixed what the screen knows and left what it looks like undesigned. E-RFINISH opened that re-design as a WU pair (W7 design, W8 build) and the design run came back saying the arrangement is wrong in six places at once — a wizard's cursor over a model that refuses to claim progress, one question asked three times by three independent target pickers, four disclosures three levels deep in a 384 px column, a re-check control drawn as if it governed a layer it cannot read, a disabled remedy button that informs nobody, and a whole second dialog body for one of three modes. That is a screen-shaped change with its own e2e spine, not debt payment. Why its own epic: the owner's call at E-RFINISH's W7 round — a rebuild of the two largest screens in the app, carrying a re-written onboarding.spec.ts, three shape nits, a missing seam test and a UC-ONBOARD re-statement, does not belong as two WUs inside an epic whose intent is pay the debt E-REDESIGN named. E-RFINISH keeps its debt and its shell/list track; this epic gets the screens.

What this epic inherits

The design work is already done and is not on any merged branch. It lives, closed and archived, on the WU branch E-RFINISH opened and dropped — that branch is the carrier, and this epic resumes from it.

Inputs — may not be re-opened, may not be narrowed

DEC-33 — two layers and a clock

Probes answer for this machine, at the moment they ran; timestamps answer for the wire, judged against a moving clock. Neither borrows. The 30 s window and the 3 s tunnel grace are settled constants. The run made the two layers the two bands — the arrangement follows the decision, it does not revisit it.

DEC-13 — what the screen may promise

A design run may not narrow a shipped capability, and its inherited assertion stands: stop routing appears only after the proxy was set. A gain is permitted; a loss is a review finding.

UC-ONBOARD — the contract

The CA save step, the copy confirms, the probed verdicts, the quit remedy. The closed run audits all twenty-three promises row by row; the build re-checks them rather than trusting the audit.

Non-goals

  • E-RFINISH's tracks. The four stale docstrings, the performance benchmark, the shell and flow-list second pass, the status-class API and the BodyViewer relabel stay where they are. This epic takes the two screens and nothing else that epic is holding.
  • Re-opening DEC-33 or DEC-13. Both are inputs. If the build finds either wrong, that is a decision record of its own, not a quiet edit.
  • Capture-core work. The one core question in reach is a timestamp on the /stats tally, and the default is that it does not land here — see below.
  • Making the screens smaller as a goal. 563 and 288 lines are the symptom the run diagnosed, not the acceptance criterion. A line count is evidence, never a target.
