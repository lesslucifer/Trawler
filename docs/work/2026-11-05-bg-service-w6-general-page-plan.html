The page sidebar, the General page and its two switches, built as W5 designed them and wired to the shell's preference, tray and login-item commands.

v1 · 2026-11-05 · Trawler · T3 (docs/work/) · WU plan · branch wu/bg-service/general-page off epic/bg-service · epic plan: E-BGSVC · design: W5's run (blob 38a949ad)

Requirement

The Settings window opens on a page sidebar (General · Capture service, ↑/↓ moves and shows). General shows keep capturing when the window closes ticked only when the shell reads it on, disabled with its reason when no menu-bar icon exists, and open Trawler at login ticked whenever a login entry exists, its program in the tooltip. Each tick writes through the shell, and a refused write shows its reason while the box shows what is stored.
