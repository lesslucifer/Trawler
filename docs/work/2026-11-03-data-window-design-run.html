Design run: the data window

A window handed one piece of data by value. It renders the data whole and lets the user edit a scratch copy, find in it and decode any value. Only find stays on screen; every other act sits in one ⋯ menu or a right-click.

Design run · closed 2026-11-04 after the owner's rounds 1 and 2 · Trawler · T3 (docs/work/) · E-INSPWIN.W2 · branch wu/inspect-window/data-window-design · epic E-INSPWIN · WU plan · components: Menu · EditSurface · FormTable · HexView · CutMarker amended in its delta · within DEC-71

Mockup

Live. Click a line, type, click away. Open ⋯ to undo, restore the original, re-indent, sort keys, fold to a depth or save. Type in find and step with ↑ ↓. Select a value (the JWT on session_token), right-click it and choose decode. Change the kind in the status bar. The frame is the window at its real size, scaled to fit; the buttons under it force one state at a time.

overflow: window header, find bar and status bar pinned, each one row that wraps as the window narrows and never scrolls sideways. surface: the one zone that scrolls, both ways, inside itself, and it is uncapped: it holds the whole payload, which the core caps at 10 MiB (DEC-71 rule 7), virtualised by EditSurface, FormTable and HexView. The decode peek opens inside the surface under the line it decodes, scrolls inside itself past 256 px (body-max) and pushes the lines below it down. Form pairs are in wire order until sort by key, a declared working-copy step that reorders the pairs themselves. menus: the ⋯ menu and the right-click menu float over every other zone, one at a time, and flip to stay inside the window. opener: the body pane, as it is in the main window: its caps and cut marker are unchanged, and the cut marker's way out is this window.

Payload is mono: the surface's lines, the form keys and values, the hex rows and the decoded result. The title is the opener's caption and stays ui.
