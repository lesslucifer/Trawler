Design run: the data window

A window handed one piece of data by value. It renders the data whole, offers the tools of its kind and the decoders, and lets the user work a scratch copy freely, with the original always one click away.

Design run · round 1 on 2026-11-03, open for the owner · Trawler · T3 (docs/work/) · E-INSPWIN.W2 · branch wu/inspect-window/data-window-design · epic E-INSPWIN · WU plan · components: EditSurface · FormTable · HexView · CutMarker amended in its delta · within DEC-68

Mockup

Live. Edit a line and click away, then undo, redo or restore the original. Reformat, sort keys, fold from the gutter or to a depth, find and step through matches. Run a JSONPath query (this stand-in reads $, .key, ..key, [n] and [*]) or a text filter. Select any text, or a form row, and press decode. Switch what copy and save deliver. The frame is the window at its real size, scaled to fit. The buttons under it force one state, or one payload, at a time.

overflow: window header, toolbar, view band and status line pinned; header and toolbar wrap onto more rows as the window narrows, never scrolling sideways. surface: the one zone that scrolls, both ways, inside itself, and it is uncapped: it holds the whole payload, which the core caps at 10 MiB (DEC-68 rule 7), virtualised by EditSurface, FormTable and HexView. Form pairs are in wire order until sort by key, a declared working-copy step that reorders the pairs themselves. decoder panel: 400 px beside the surface (stacked under it below 900 px). It scrolls inside itself, and each result is capped by the pane member it mounts (JsonRenderer 128 KiB, HexRenderer 4 KiB); open in data window is the way past. opener: the body pane, as it is in the main window: its caps and cut marker are unchanged, and the cut marker's way out is this window.

Payload is mono: the surface's lines, the form keys and values, the hex rows and the decoded result. The title is the opener's caption and stays ui.
