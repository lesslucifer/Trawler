Deviations

#DeviationWhy, and what it cost
1 The emulator needs no reach at all. The plan predicted the emulator and the device would differ in their steps; they differ in whether the epic's whole seam applies. 10.0.2.2 is the alias every AVD resolves to the machine it runs on, so the emulator reaches a loopback-bound Trawler already this is the run's blocked-on row 3 answered rather than restated — it handed the build the instruction content and said the draft must be confirmed. Confirming it meant deciding that arming reach for an emulator would be an exposure the platform does not need. Cost: none; it is one row of a table, and the test asserts the two platforms differ in reach, in certificate route and in host
2 The UC re-check found three losses, and they were real. The plan treated the re-check as a verification step. It was the WU's last commit: the manual guide had stopped rendering inline where this machine has no one-click path, had stopped opening itself when the action it backs up fails, and the idle verdict had stopped dating the last flow all three are promises the mockup did not render and so did not carry — a mockup shows one state, and three states it never showed were where the contract lived. This is the argument for the epic's own rule (re-checked against the tree rather than trusted from the run's audit) paying for itself inside one WU. Restored, each with a test that names the promise
3 The verdict zone renders seven readings, not the run's four. The mockup draws checking, nothing yet, arriving but not decrypting and capturing. The screen also renders nothing is arriving now, arriving over plain HTTP only and a connection just arrived two of the three are the run's own requirement: its Given list says traffic.ever "separates nothing has ever arrived from nothing is arriving now — they are different problems", which is two sentences where the mockup drew one. The third, plain-HTTP, is a DEC-33 reading the run's Given list omits; the screen must say something true when it happens, and "nothing has reached Trawler yet" would be false. Recorded here because it is the one sentence on the screen the design of record does not contain
4 DEC-33's module moved with three of its sentences rewritten, where the plan said the move was pure. Two probe remedies pointed at "step 3", which no longer exists; the un-probeable short became can't check on this platform; and the arriving remedy became the design's own sentence. trafficStatus also gained a trafficReading underneath it the wording was not behaviour, and leaving copy that names a step the screen no longer has would have been dead-but-wrong data. The refactor underneath is what lets the screen and the precondition word one reading two ways without either comparing verdict strings — which is how a rename would have silently unwired a zone. The recency logic, its constants and its precedence are unchanged and their tests moved untouched
5 The reversal survives a probe that cannot answer. The plan said stop routing appears once the proxy probe reads ours. It also appears when the set action just succeeded DEC-13's reversal symmetry is the stronger rule: an action that changed this host may not become irreversible because a probe failed. Both halves are asserted
6 Three small composition calls the mockup does not settle. The try zone renders a caption where a command follows it and a Notice where the sentence is the zone's whole content (the mockup uses a caption for the Simulator); the save control is the mockup's labelled save… button rather than the ↓ IconButton UC-ONBOARD names; the probe row's stamp is relative (checked 2 minutes ago) per the mockup, where UC-ONBOARD says checked at HH:MM:SS the first is the screen's own rule, inside both component contracts. The second and third are the design of record overriding prose the UC wrote before it — which is what a design of record is for. All three are W4's to restate in docs/use-cases/web-ui.html; none changes what the user can do
7 Notice tone="pending" is used more widely than the run proposed. The run proposed it for one zone — the checking verdict. The screen also uses it for every in-flight action note, replacing tone="mute" the proposal's argument is that a pending zone rendered mute reads as settled. That is equally true of "installing — approve the system prompt…", which is the same nothing in the same anatomy. The component contract carries it; the extension is not widened by the second caller
8 The screen grew: 563 → 831 lines, while the instruction data left it for setup.ts stated because the epic's non-goals name the number explicitly — "563 lines is the symptom the run diagnosed, not the acceptance criterion; a line count is evidence, never a target". What arrived is the reach precondition, the per-platform branching of three zones, and the seven-reading verdict table; what left is every instruction string. The evidence this WU offers instead of a line count is that the screen no longer asks one question three times
9 ui/e2e/specs/onboarding.spec.ts is stale at this tip and was left that way, as the plan said it would be it asserts a stepper that no longer exists. W4 rewrites it against the platform axis with a coverage map over its 8 assertions; editing it here would have landed a spec asserting a screen nobody designed. The gate does not run it — the WU's suite is the Vitest suite and cargo test --all, both green
