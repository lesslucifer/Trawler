Proposed inline summary per note

What each chip shows without opening the modal — the smallest thing that tells you "is this note here, and is it healthy":

noteinline chipshows
testtest(1/5/w)unit/integration/e2e — counts, w=waived, –=layer absent. Your idea, one glance at coverage across the three levels.
crcr ⚑3verdict shown by color (green=PASS, red=FAIL) — no text; plus findings count as a flag.
docdoc(5)how many docs changed. Tier breakdown moved to the modal — keeps the chip short.
doddod ✓6the green receipt: a checkmark + how many gate checks passed. Its presence is the signal the WU is done.
cr —a ref with no note on that commit: dashed, muted, not clickable.
