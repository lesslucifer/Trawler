What the mark must carry

What Trawler is

A native MITM debugging proxy: it sits between your apps and the network, decrypts HTTPS with a local CA, and shows every request and response. It trawls traffic.

Its UI today

Warm dark, zero accent (DEC-19): canvas #2b2622, ink #f7f5f0, mute #aea69c. Inter, DM Mono, and the title Trawler in Instrument Serif italic.

Rejected round 0

Net (A), serif T (B), hook + packet with a green dot (C). None of those motifs come back here.
