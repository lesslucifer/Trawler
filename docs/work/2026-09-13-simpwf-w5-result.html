What it saves, stated honestly

The plan called the saving informative rather than a gate, and the number is worth stating plainly because it is not the number the epic plan implied.

QuestionWhat the corpus says
How many rounds did a nit alone buy? None. E-RFINISH's rounds were bought by importants (W6's five), by false sentences in documents (W9's eleven, W2's one) or by defects inside the previous round's fix (W3's, W6's). Under today's rule a nit could not survive to buy a round: it either failed the tip or was waived.
Then what does W5 change? It removes a class of round before it can be bought, and it improves the record. Today's binary rule pressures a verifier toward false-positive for anything small — W6 round 1 refuted seven of ten candidates, among them "the three test doubles should be one parameterized builder" and "shellApi duplicates mockApi". Those are nit-shaped judgments that had to be refuted because the only other exit was failing the tip. After this WU they can be recorded as what they are.
What does the local tally show? This epic's own six gated tips (cargo xtask tally): every refusal so far is 8·manual-review or 1·notes — paperwork, not findings. W2's preflight is what addresses those; W5 is not measurable there.

The measured saving in this corpus is zero rounds. The rule is adopted on recall holding and on the class of round it forecloses, not on a round it is shown to have removed. Stated here rather than rounded up, because the epic's whole discipline is that cuts are chosen from counts.
