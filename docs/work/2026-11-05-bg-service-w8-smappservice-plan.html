E-BGSVC.W8 — the login item through SMAppService

On macOS the login item is registered with Background Task Management itself, so ticking and unticking show in System Settings at once.

v1 · 2026-11-05 · Trawler · T3 (docs/work/) · WU plan · branch wu/bg-service/login-item-smappservice off epic/bg-service · fixes W4's macOS entry; W6's page stays as signed

Requirement

On macOS, from the packaged Trawler.app, writing the login item on registers the bundled launch agent dev.trawler.app.plist with SMAppService, and off unregisters it; Background Task Management lists or drops the entry immediately, named “Trawler”. At login the agent starts the app's binary with --background. Reading reports on when the service status is enabled or awaiting approval. A refusal surfaces as the box's “couldn't save — …”. From a binary with no bundle the item is unavailable and says so.
