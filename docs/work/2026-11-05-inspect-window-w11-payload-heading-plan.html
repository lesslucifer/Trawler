E-INSPWIN.W11 — the payload carries its heading; years below 100

The window's header reads what the opener hands it, not a regex over its title; a time in the years 0–99 reads true.

v2 (the owner's rulings: further review fixes land here, not in new WUs; main resynced) · 2026-11-05 · Trawler · T3 WU plan · E-INSPWIN.W11 · branch wu/inspect-window/payload-heading · from the epic review's round-2 FAIL on c90ec373 · owner's ruling 2026-11-05: structured heading · epic plan (archived)

Requirement

(1) An opener may hand the data window an optional heading {what, method, host, path, query} beside title; the shell carries it unread; the window's header draws from it, and from the title alone when there is none — no part of the title is parsed. (2) The time decoder reads a date or date-time in the years 0000–0099 on the proleptic Gregorian calendar: its zone offset is the zone's, and 0000-02-29 is a day.

Mapping

E-INSPWIN · UC-9 (the window's caption; decoder rows, time) · DEC-76 rule 1 amended (the payload) · the epic review's two verified importants on c90ec373: the caption's format held by a writer (bodyPayload) and a regex (captionParts); Date.UTC mapping years 0–99 to 1900–1999.
