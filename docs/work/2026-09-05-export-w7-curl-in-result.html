Deviations

One, a narrowed claim. The plan's expected result 7 and the epic's expected result 8 say any flow rendered by toCurl parses back equal. Writing the property found the two shapes where that cannot hold, and the parser is right to fail them because curl itself would:

ShapeWhat toCurl writesWhat curl — and the parser — does with it
a header with a blank value-H 'Name: '"send no such header" — dropped. The value that is sent empty is spelled Name;, which toCurl does not write
a GET carrying a bodyno -X, plus --data-rawa POST

The loss is in the serializer's output as curl reads it, not in the parser, so the parser was not bent to match; the generator excludes the two shapes, the exclusion is stated beside it, and DEC-32 carries the boundary. Nothing else deviated: every row of the plan's accepted, ignored and refusal tables shipped as written, the module lives where the plan put it, the body travels raw, and the refusal is W3's plain text.

Follow-ups
