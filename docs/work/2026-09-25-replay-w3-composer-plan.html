Requirement

In the app, a developer opens a draft from the flow in the inspector (replay) or from nothing (new request), edits its method, URL, headers and body in the detail pane, and sends it. Only what differs from the seed reaches POST /flows/{id}/replay, and a draft from scratch reaches POST /flows. The outcome (the status, the duration and open the new flow, or ⚠ with the core's sentence) lands under the send button. The list marks the new row ↗ sent, and the one draft is kept until another replaces it.
