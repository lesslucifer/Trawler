E-ONBRVP.W21 — notes on one switch

The setup screen carries a ? at the end of every line something explains: 33 of them across the platforms. Seven explain nothing worth a click, and the rest crowd the lines they sit on. This WU drops the eight that do not earn their place and puts every note that stays behind one switch on the device row.

Requirement

The setup screen shows one ?, in a circle, at the right end of the device row: a switch that shows or hides every note the screen carries, each under the line it explains. The choice holds across platforms, screens and launches, and starts hidden. The quit dialog's notes follow the same choice. The eight notes ruled out below are gone from every platform.
