Two findings, not one.

1 · The cut loses real findings. L2 is raised by a dedicated finder and missed by the merged one, on the same diff, from the same rubric text. One attributable loss is all the stopping rule needs, and a blocker is the worst kind to lose.

2 · The bar the plan wrote cannot be met by the incumbent either. Four of the five probes show the incumbent fleet failing the same bar on a fresh run. A round's findings are a sample, not a fixed set the rubric determines — so every known-real finding is raised is a bar no configuration of this review passes, including the one in production. The A/B was built to choose between two shapes and can only reject; it cannot endorse. That is a defect in the plan's method, found by running it.

The shortfall concentrates on the largest diffs (D 21→9, G 19→3, A 14→9) and vanishes on the smallest (H 3→4, F 10→11), which is the signature of a per-context attention budget rather than of a rubric that stopped asking a question.

The mechanism the plan did not price. The plan's risk table named overlap as recall — two sweeps overlap less than five — and it was right about the direction. But the pooled candidates show zero exact duplicates between the two finders across all nine cases: the overlap the cut expected to harvest as a saving did not exist to harvest. What the five finders were buying was not redundant coverage of the same claims; it was five separate attention budgets. A finder returns its best n candidates, and n is a property of the context, not of how many rubric rows it was handed. Folding five rows into two contexts halved the round's output and left the rubric text untouched — which is exactly why nothing in the rubric diff predicted it.

The diagnosis, and the re-measurement not taken

The plan permits one re-measurement against a diagnosis, and names the two causes an escape can have with the answer each gets:

Cause the plan namedIts prescribed answerDoes it fit?
A verifier refuted a real claim — the batch is too largeLower the cap below 12 No. Zero known-real candidates were refuted. Every loss is at the finder stage
A finder never raised the claim — the grouping is wrongThree finders, splitting shape Fits the symptom, cannot reach the cause. Splitting shape leaves behaviour untouched, and behaviour is where L2 — the one escape the control arm attributes to the cut — was lost. The prescribed lever cannot recover the killing finding

The re-measurement was not taken, and the reason is recorded here rather than left implicit. Its only reachable outcome was the one already in hand: shape splits, L2 stays lost, the cut drops. Against that, the control arm answered the question the re-measurement was for — is the escape the cut's fault? — at five agents instead of a second full corpus run. The owner can order the three-finder run on this evidence; nothing here forecloses it, and --fleet is now in the tree to measure it.

The deeper reading is that three finders would be the same bet at a smaller stake. If the budget is per context, recall tracks the finder count, and the epic's question stops being how few finders and becomes what does the fifth finder cost, and what does it return — which is the question tally --fleet was built to answer and now can.

The instrument that stayed

The fleet: block costs the gate nothing — no check reads it, a missing block is a reading not taken rather than a fault — so the plan kept it on the rejection path. It is the epic's promise that the next cuts are chosen from counts, and after this WU there is nothing else in the tree that can count a round.
