Deviations

The router's message replaced the brief's three directions with one direction on the owner's picks; the doc carried that one plus two log variations (round 4 keeps V1). Three calls made here: the privacy headline is PRIVACY.md's “Trawler keeps your traffic on your machine.”, not “Nothing leaves your machine” (false: the update check and the forwarded traffic leave); the router's sample “in seconds” is dropped (no measured speed); amber is used as glow only, which needs a DEC-89 rule 6 amendment W5 writes (owner mr commit). The hero, privacy band, footer and 404 are dark in both themes, so wordmark-light.png is no longer used. Headings are weight 700: the app's one-weight rule binds ui/, not the site. cargo xtask design-scope finds no design artifact, so no design/v1 note is owed. Docs-only WU: the test layers are waived; W2 and W3 carry the tests this design specifies.
