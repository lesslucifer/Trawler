Test plan

This WU changes no code path — src/, xtask/, tests/, ui/src/ and ui/e2e/ are all untouched — but it is not exempt: a design is a docs-only deliverable, so the gate keeps a design-bearing diff gated on purpose. The evidence it owes and does not owe is therefore decided per layer, not by the docs-only shortcut.

LevelWhat asserts what
Unit Waived — waived: "the WU adds no executable code; its only artifact is a design run doc, and every predicate over that doc is already a checker rule." The claim is not that the work is untested: verify-doc is the test, and it is a suite that already exists and already runs on every write and at every turn end. What could go wrong, and the answer

RiskAnswer
The run was written under the checker as it stood on 2026-09-17 and parked as a draft, so every rule has been silent on it since. It may be red in ways the first round never saw — E-SIMPDF re-homed three rules onto the mockup doc after it was parked expected, and budgeted. The first verify-doc after the restore is a discovery run, not a regression. Its output is the work list; residuals are batched into a second pass
Amending the mockup means re-mounting real components against the current registry. An entry's @use/@avoid may have moved since the run was written, making a mount that was a reuse into a proposal the substrate is re-loaded at the start of the round (node scripts/design-registry.mjs), not assumed from the parked doc. A fit that no longer holds becomes a proposals row, which is exactly what that list is for
Amending for reach touches the same zones the first round's human already approved, and quietly changing a settled call would spend a judgment nobody made the amendment is scoped to the reach seam. Anything it forces outside that scope is raised to the human as a call in its own right and recorded in the review item with the contract it hit — the run's review list is append-only
The bill is the epic's deliverable and is trivially lost — no round in the corpus has ever recorded one the clock starts at the restore and the token count is read at the close; both land in this WU's result before the mr signature, because the report's bytes are what the human signs
