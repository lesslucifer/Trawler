Requirement

W2 delivers one design run at docs/work/2026-11-04-query-bar-design-run.html and one component design at docs/work/components/query-bar.html. The run mounts the real header and flow-list components around a live query bar whose chips filter the mockup's rows. It draws every chip kind (keyed, text, negated, refused by the core), the draft and its fault, and the suggestion list with its highlighted entry. It gives a key path for every pointer act, and lists the owner's calls. The owner reviews it; it is closed in three steps (review · green · data-status="archived"), and a design/v1 note on the tip names both artifacts.
