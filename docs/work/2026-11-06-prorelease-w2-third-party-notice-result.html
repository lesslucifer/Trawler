Licence compatibility

Every licence is compatible with distributing Trawler under MIT: permissive (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC, Zlib, BSL-1.0, Unlicense, 0BSD, BlueOak-1.0.0, CDLA-Permissive-2.0, Unicode-3.0), file-level MPL-2.0 used unmodified (cssparser, cssparser-macros, selectors, dtoa-short, option-ext), OFL-1.1 fonts bundled with software, and r-efi's LGPL taken under its MIT option. No GPL/AGPL-only dependency. Detail: DEC-88 § Compatibility.
