The doc the plan expected to touch, and did not

The plan allowed documentation-system.html one line, and only if D3·E contradicts what it already says about where a design artifact lives. Read end to end: it says nothing about component designs having a home. Designs are T3 under docs/work/ like every other in-flight artifact, its distillation rule already sends UI-touching work to design-rules/, and its tracked-location rule already forbids anything under docs/work/ at an epic tip.

That is D3·E's whole point restated: the address problem was never a missing location, it was DEC-16 granting an unbuilt design the right to survive its epic. Remove the right and the documentation system needs no new row — which is the evidence that the ruling was right, so it is recorded here rather than silently skipped.
