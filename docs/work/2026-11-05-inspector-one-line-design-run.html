Design run: the inspector's one-line values

Every header value and the URI sit on one line, cut at the end when they are wider than the pane. The URI opens whole in a data window; the Headers section opens all of the leg's headers whole in one. The body says when its bytes overruled the served type.

Design run · closed 2026-11-05 after the owner's round 1 (accept all) · Trawler · T3 (docs/work/) · E-INSPWIN2.W2 · branch wu/inspect-window-v2/inspector-design · epic E-INSPWIN2 · WU plan · within DEC-78 and DEC-79

Mockup

Live. Click a value to copy it, click ↗ beside the URI or on the Headers heading to see what the data window would be handed, switch the leg. The frame is the detail pane at its default 520 px, scaled to fit; the buttons under it force one state at a time.

overflow: identity bar and flow line pinned; the URI is one line, cut at the end with an ellipsis when wider than the pane (DEC-78), and the pane never scrolls sideways. headers: capped at the user's headers share of the pane (DEC-32, DEC-67) and scrolls inside itself, down only; each value is one line, cut at the end, its whole reached through the heading's ↗ open all; names are never cut. body: takes the rest and scrolls inside itself, uncapped by this run (BodyViewer's caps and cut marker are unchanged).

Payload is mono: the URI, header names and values, the body. The served as … statement is ui chrome.
