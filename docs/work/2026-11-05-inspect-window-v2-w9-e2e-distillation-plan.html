Journeys

#Asserts
J1a flow with a 4 KiB set-cookie, a JWT authorization and a 2 KiB URI: each value's box is one line high and ends cut; neither the headers zone nor the document scrolls sideways; a click copies the whole value; open all hands the response's headers whole and in wire order; the request's JWT decodes in its window to header and payload; open on the URI hands the whole URI; a second open all focuses the first window
J2JSON served as text/plain: the pane states JSON · served as text/plain and draws no text body, the window reads from its bytes as JSON; HTML served as application/octet-stream: html in the pane, the window offers the preview; {oops as text/plain stays text in both
J3a minified script opens as javascript, find counts a seeded identifier, reformat adds lines and one edit, undo returns to unmodified, reformat then restore original copies the served bytes
J4control: the same page, unguarded, reaches the beacon by script, image, stylesheet and refresh; its preview makes none of those requests; for a page with an anchor, a MathML and an SVG link, the real engine's :any-link over the preview's markup finds only anchors aimed at a context the sandbox withholds, over the unguarded page it finds the links
J5typing ke offers key:, taking it offers the document's keys, a chosen key makes a chip with its matches counted; a quoted phrase with a space makes a second chip, the count is the sum and Enter steps across both; re:( makes no chip; Backspace removes the last chip; switching to text refuses the key: chip; the flow list's filter in the main window still makes chips and narrows the list
