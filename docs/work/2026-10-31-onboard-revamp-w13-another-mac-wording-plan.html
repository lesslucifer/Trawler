Waiting for your rulings on thirteen findings, two handed-in rows, one noticed row and four questions. Found by reading the Another Mac reels at the epic tip (9160b1d8: macos-mac, windows-mac, linux-mac, filmed 2026-10-31 after the last UI change) and its guide's source. Nothing is built yet.

Requirement

On Another Mac, on every host, each state of the setup screen leads with the one thing to do next and shows the optional, the explanatory and the done as secondary. Every word names one thing one way and is true on the host it is shown on. Each by-hand step on that Mac offers a route without a terminal where one exists, with verified media, and keeps its command as another way. Every way back undoes every way in. A test holds each rule.
