The column handles stay W4's
a pane width is rounded to whole pixels as it is setreview round 1 (correctness, blocker): a drag under display scaling gives fractional pixels, which the shell's integer field refused, and the write was lost silently
splitter B's value is the share the headers actually take, at most the ceilingreview round 1 (correctness, important): a short leg sits below its ceiling, and a drag starting from the ceiling did not follow the pointer
