Graphics need 3:1: every stroke and accent passes. A tile against a same-tone background is low by nature, so each tile carries a faint edge line. Amber is two tones: #e0a43a on dark tiles, #a8690a on paper.

Recommendation

5b4 Aperture · ink + amber · sans wordmark. The only idea you loved in both rounds; its straight bars snap to the pixel grid, so it stays the sharpest at 16 px and as a menu-bar glyph; the amber slot marks the one place Trawler sits (3.9:1 on paper, above the 3:1 bar for graphics) and the light tile stands out from the dark app and dark Docks. Ink-only stays the mono fallback (tray, print). The sans wordmark is Inter, the app's own UI font.
