Where the Frame does real work

Two lines carry this run. Both are load-bearing because they forbid something the screen would otherwise reach for.

Not given — the app cannot observe per-step completion. A step is proven by a probe or by captured traffic, never by the stepper's cursor. That is why StepList's @avoid already refuses per-step completion and a current-step highlight, and why StatusLine's refuses a checkbox rendering: "a tick lies in most real states." The Frame states the absence; the components enforce it. A design that draws a tick per step has broken the Frame, not just a rule.

The two surfaces share a Frame's constraints but not its goals. Setup is a screen the user works through once; the dialog is a two-second interruption at quit, where the top goal is do not strand the machine without internet. One run, two Frames — and the run says so rather than averaging them into one driver table.

Outcome — what the run found

The run closed at design run #9. Three things the plan could only forecast:

The short gap is measured4 of 16 verdicts exceed StatusLine's ~40-char contract (101, 116, 63, 63 chars), and the two error verdicts interpolate without bound. All 16 short forms are specified in the run; four remedies move out of the verdict into a Notice.
One new registry componentNine entries reused across both surfaces. The modal shell stays build local on the sample-of-one warrant; the checkbox became a Checkbox T1 registry propose at review — a primitive whose contract generalises from one instance, unlike a two-mode modal. It needs its own component-design WU before W10 can build the dialog's remote mode.
The dialog's mode model was wrong, and the review caught itunset_proxy clears whatever proxy the host has, so a foreign proxy was always as fixable as ours — the first pass gave it read-only instructions. Three modes now key on fixability, not ownership: ours and foreign both offer the action plus a collapsed manual route; only remote is info-only, and it alone carries the opt-out.
Two slugs do not resolveconfirm-at-point is satisfied-via-proposal (contingent on short landing) and focus-returns-to-origin is blocked — modality is genuinely unowned until W10 builds the shell. Neither is claimed as satisfied.
