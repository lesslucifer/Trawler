Why this epic exists

After E1 (the workflow standard) and E2 (doc archive layout) merged, workflow-tooling maintenance kept landing as standalone commits on main. Each was individually legal under the direct-to-main rule, but the stream is one continuing line of work and belongs under an epic: plan first, WU gates, epic-scope review. This epic is that line's home, and it adopts the stream: main was rewound (pointer only — no rewrite, all shas and notes intact) to a9cd230, so the four commits sit inside the epic range and the epic walk re-verifies each as a standalone commit at the merge into main.
